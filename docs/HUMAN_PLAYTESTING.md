# Human playtesting

Human testing begins only after machine filters can curate a small set. Each
candidate presentation must include complete derived rules, measured fairness by
available strength, asymmetry and simplicity summaries, known weaknesses, and a
minimal way to play.

Prefer small comparative prompts such as “which is simpler?” over broad numeric
ratings. Ask testers to switch roles. Preserve raw observations separately for:
comprehension time, perceived simplicity, perceived fairness, role preference,
strategic interest, replay desire, and free-form confusion. Derived weights must
remain traceable to raw evidence.

No external participants, public release, or personal-data collection occurs
without a human permission checkpoint and an appropriate consent/privacy design.
